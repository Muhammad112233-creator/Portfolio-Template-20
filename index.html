THE USUAL THING

Lots of motion.
Not much momentum.

  • ×Meet the account lead. Then the designer. Then someone new.
  • ×A polished deck, three weeks before the first real screen.
  • ×“Out of scope” arrives just when things get interesting.
  • ×Five opinions in the room. None of them are yours.
  • ×Looks familiar. Feels familiar. Gets forgotten.
OR
✳
A GOODFIELD PROJECT

Good people.
Good things, made.

  • ✓The person you talk to is the person making the work.
  • ✓Something useful to react to early. Then we make it better.
  • ✓A clear scope and an honest answer if it changes.
  • ✓Fewer meetings. More decisions made together.
  • ✓A thoughtful point of view, shaped around your people.
Less hand-off. More hands-on. ↗The process, in four steps ↗
